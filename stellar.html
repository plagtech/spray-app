Why Spraay on Stellar?

Protocol-native batch payments with ~5 second finality.

⚛️

Native Batching

Stellar supports up to 100 operations per transaction at the protocol level. No smart contract needed — batching is built in.

⚡

5-Second Finality

Transactions settle in approximately 5 seconds on Stellar. No waiting for block confirmations.

💸

Near-Zero Fees

Stellar network fees are ~0.00001 XLM per operation. A 99-recipient batch costs less than a fraction of a cent.

🔐

Non-Custodial

Spraay builds the transaction, your wallet signs it. Your keys never leave your browser. Atomic execution — all or nothing.

🌐

x402 Native

Stellar has first-class x402 support with OpenZeppelin facilitators. Spraay is x402-native on every chain.

📋

CSV Import

Upload a CSV with addresses and amounts. Perfect for payroll, airdrops, grants, and bulk distributions.

How It Works

Four steps. One transaction. Atomic execution.

1

Connect Wallet

Connect your Freighter or Albedo wallet. Your secret key stays local.

2

Add Recipients

Enter Stellar addresses (G...) and XLM amounts, or upload a CSV file.

3

Review & Sign

Spraay builds a multi-op transaction. Your wallet signs it locally.

4

Submit

Signed transaction is submitted to Stellar. Settles in ~5 seconds.
