How It Works

Four steps. One transaction. Non-custodial.

1

Connect Wallet

Connect your UniSat or Xverse browser wallet. Your keys stay local.

2

Add Recipients

Enter Bitcoin addresses and amounts (in BTC), or upload a CSV file.

3

Sign PSBT

We construct a PSBT. Your wallet signs it locally — non-custodial.

4

Broadcast

Signed transaction is broadcast to the network. Track on mempool.space.
