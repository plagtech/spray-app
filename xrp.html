Why Spraay on XRP?

Non-custodial batch payments with near-zero fees and instant finality.

🔐

Non-Custodial

Your wallet signs each transaction locally. Private keys never leave your device. GemWallet, Crossmark, or Xaman.

⚡

3-4 Second Finality

XRP Ledger validates transactions in 3-4 seconds. No waiting for block confirmations — payments settle almost instantly.

💰

Near-Zero Fees

XRP network fees are ~0.000012 XRP per transaction (~$0.00003). Even 100 recipients costs fractions of a cent.

🏷️

Destination Tags

Per-recipient destination tags supported — critical for sending to exchanges and hosted wallets that require them.

📋

CSV Import

Upload a CSV with addresses, amounts, and optional destination tags. Perfect for payroll, airdrops, and bulk distributions.

🌐

Multi-Chain

Spraay is live on 15+ chains including Base, Ethereum, Solana, Bitcoin, Stellar, and now XRP Ledger.

How It Works

Four steps. Sequential signing. Non-custodial.

1

Connect Wallet

Connect GemWallet, Crossmark, or Xaman. Your keys stay local on your device.

2

Add Recipients

Enter XRP addresses, amounts, and optional destination tags. Or upload a CSV file.

3

Sign Transactions

Spraay builds sequential Payment transactions. Your wallet signs each one locally.

4

Watch It Settle

Each payment validates in 3-4 seconds. Track on the XRP Ledger explorer.
